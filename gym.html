12 Week Summer Bulk

Upper Body Specialization Plan

A 5-day hypertrophy-focused gym plan built around maximizing upper-body muscle while keeping legs to once per week. Priorities: side delts, upper chest, lats, arms, rear delts, and enough recovery to actually grow.

5 Days Training per week
2 Days Full rest per week
170–190g Protein target per day
+0.25–0.5kg Target gain per week

Full Rest
No lifting today. This is where a lot of the actual growth happens. Keep protein high, stay hydrated, and prepare for Monday.

Progression + Execution Rules

Simple rules to make the plan work for the full 12 weeks.

Follow These

Progressive Overload

  • Use the listed rep ranges.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, increase weight.
  • Example: for 3 × 8–12, once you hit 12/12/12, go heavier.
  • Small jumps are fine. Progress does not need to be dramatic every week.

Effort Level

  • Compounds: stop with 1–2 reps in reserve.
  • Isolation lifts: stop with 0–1 reps in reserve.
  • Do not fail heavy bench, rows, shoulder press, squats, or RDLs often.
  • Failure is more acceptable on raises, curls, pushdowns, flies, and rear delts.

Deload

  • Weeks 1–4: build volume and technique.
  • Weeks 5–7: push progression hard.
  • Week 8: deload with about half the sets and lighter weights.
  • Weeks 9–12: push hard again.

Nutrition

  • Protein: 170–190g/day.
  • Calories: maintenance + 300–500/day.
  • Goal gain: 0.25–0.5kg/week.
  • If weight is not moving after 2 weeks, add 150–250 calories/day.

Keep form clean, progress slowly, and prioritize recovery. Shoulder or elbow pain means reduce volume before it becomes an injury.
