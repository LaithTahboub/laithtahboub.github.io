Research

I'm interested in computer vision, generative models and 3D. Most of my research is about synthesizing new views of real places from a few casual photos taken in the wild, by different people, at different times and in different conditions.

Tour the World: Consistent Novel View Synthesis from a Few Wild Photos
Laith Tahboub, Vinayak Gupta, Hadi Alzayer, Jia-Bin Huang
Preprint coming soon

Given a handful of internet photos of a landmark, taken years apart in different light, weather and seasons, we post-train a camera-controlled video world model to render a long, freely moving tour of the place that stays faithful to every photo and keeps one coherent look.

Coupled Diffusion Sampling for Novel View Synthesis in the Wild
Laith Tahboub, Hadi Alzayer, Jia-Bin Huang
Research project, 2026
code

Building on coupled diffusion sampling, we sample a multi-view diffusion model jointly with a pose-conditioned 2D diffusion model fine-tuned on MegaScenes, so that the 3D model keeps the geometry consistent while the 2D model handles changes in lighting and transient objects.

LLM Alignment

 Measuring LLM Alignment to Normative Text Corpora
Undergraduate research team led by Laith Tahboub, advised by Mohammed Nayeem Teli
University of Maryland, December 2024 to June 2025

We generated over 100,000 question-answer pairs from philosophical, legal and religious texts to benchmark how well large language models (GPT-4, Claude, Llama 3) align with them, and found that top models are consistently more factually aligned than morally aligned.
